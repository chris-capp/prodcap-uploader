ProdcapUploader

The tool Prodcap uses to post its own 60-second mixing lessons. Each video is watched and approved by hand, then goes out on its scheduled day.

Watch on TikTok Watch on YouTube

How a video gets posted

Nothing is posted automatically. A person signs off on every video, and the settings are theirs.

  1. Make

    A new lesson is rendered with its cover, title and caption.

  2. Review

    The creator watches it, edits the text, and picks who can view it and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are on.

  3. Approve

    It gets the next day with nothing else scheduled on TikTok or YouTube.

  4. Post

    On that day it uploads to Prodcap's own accounts with exactly the settings chosen.

What it can access

Only Prodcap's own accounts are connected. Sign-in happens on TikTok's and Google's own pages, and tokens stay on Prodcap's computer.

Uses

  • Your display name and avatar, to show which account a video goes to
  • The privacy and interaction options TikTok allows for your account
  • Permission to upload and publish the videos you approve
  • Dates already scheduled on YouTube, so days never double up

Never touches

  • Followers, likes or messages
  • Analytics or viewing history
  • Anyone else's videos or accounts
  • Your password

The lessons

Short, visual explainers on one mixing tool at a time.
