Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Prodcap Uploader (the "App"), operated by Prodcap, collects when you connect your TikTok or YouTube account, how it is used, and the choices you have.

In short: the App uses your connected accounts only to publish the videos you approve. It keeps the minimum needed to do that, on the operator's own computer. It does not sell, share or analyze your data, and it shows no ads.

1. Information we collect

From TikTok, with your permission (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish):

  • Basic profile information: your account identifier (open_id), display name and avatar, used to show which account a video will post to
  • Your posting options from TikTok's creator info (allowed privacy levels and whether comments, Duet or Stitch are turned off), used to show only the settings you're allowed to choose
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used to upload and publish on your behalf
  • Upload status and post identifiers for the videos you publish

From YouTube, with your permission (YouTube Data API scopes for uploading and managing your videos):

  • Your channel identifier and the scheduled publish dates of your existing videos, used to avoid scheduling two videos on the same day
  • OAuth tokens, and the IDs and status of videos the App uploads

Content you provide: the videos, thumbnails, titles, captions, descriptions, tags and post settings you approve.

We do not collect your password, contacts, messages, followers, viewing history, location or payment information.

2. How we use it

  • To upload and publish the videos you approve, at the times you schedule, with the settings you choose
  • To show you which account you're posting to and what settings are available
  • To confirm whether a post succeeded, and to show its status in the App

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or training AI models, and we do not sell or rent it.

3. Sharing

Your information is sent only to the platform it belongs to: TikTok for TikTok posts, and Google/YouTube for YouTube uploads. It is shared with no one else, except where required by law.

4. Storage, security and retention

Tokens and post records are stored locally on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by that user account. They are not stored on any public server. Tokens are kept until you disconnect your account or they expire. Video records are kept so the App can show your posting history. We will delete them on request.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → remove Prodcap Uploader
  • Disconnect YouTube: remove the App at myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Delete your data: email us, and we will delete stored tokens and records within 30 days

6. Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

7. TikTok

TikTok's handling of your data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
